- **Nudge** — [nudge.apk](https://chhat2206.github.io/apps/nudge.apk), build 2, 2026-11-27: Nudge: seven fixes from a review of the phone side. A task that came before notifications were allowed (Android 13+ starts with them off) was counted as nudged and never shown; now it rings as soon as they are allowed, also from Settings (the app nudges again on every return). Nudges are alarm clocks, so every 5 minutes holds while the phone dozes (the other kind is let through only every ~9 minutes). An answer from the Pi older than a tick just sent no longer brings the ticked task back. Done on a notification can no longer hold Android's receiver past its limit. A place allowed in Settings is watched at once. A second Save where I am is no longer cut short by the first one's timeout. A send that keeps failing waits longer each time, up to 2 minutes, instead of every 15 s for ever. Proof: compiles against android.jar, unit tests 13/13, the two-phone drive passes.
